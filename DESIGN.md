# GlagahSmart visual direction

GlagahSmart is a field dossier for turning glagah residue into a defensible next step.

- **Identity:** agricultural fieldwork meets a small materials lab; practical, observant, locally grounded.
- **Palette:** warm paper and ink as the canvas, forest green for decisions, clay orange for uncertainty or heat, and restrained mint for verified states.
- **Typography:** compact sans-serif for measurements and navigation; a serif display face for the human research voice.
- **Composition:** editorial asymmetry and evidence rails instead of generic hero + equal card grids. Each section should answer one research question.
- **Motion:** quiet transitions only. Motion marks a state change or navigation response; it does not decorate every block.
- **Content rule:** distinguish measured, simulated, and planned work. Never invent outcomes, users, or performance numbers.
